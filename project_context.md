Overview

eSanjeevani is an online healthcare platform that allows patients to consult doctors from anywhere using the internet. It makes healthcare more accessible, convenient, and efficient for everyone.

Purpose

The platform aims to connect patients and doctors easily, reduce the need for hospital visits, and provide quick medical support, especially for people in remote areas.

Key Features
Online doctor consultation
Easy appointment booking
Digital prescriptions
Access from anywhere
Secure patient data
🤖 AI Features
Smart symptom checking to guide patients
AI-based suggestions for basic health concerns
Chatbot support for instant help and queries
Improved appointment recommendations
Faster and more accurate assistance
Benefits
Saves time and travel effort
Reduces hospital crowd
Helpful for rural and remote users
Quick access to medical advice
Use Case

A patient can book an appointment, consult a doctor online, and also get initial guidance through AI-based tools before the consultation.

Conclusion

eSanjeevani combines digital healthcare with AI support to make medical services more accessible, faster, and user-friendly.
